Our story

Miyaru means shark.

In Dhivehi, miyaru is the shark — calm, efficient, completely at home in the water. That's the standard we set for our gear.

We're a small crew of Maldivian divers who got tired of bulky kit, fogged-up masks and being stranded when the air ran low. So we built our own: a pocket cylinder you refill yourself, masks tuned for our reefs, and fins that reward a slow, patient kick. Everything is tested where we live — between the channels and coral of the Maldives — before it ever reaches you.
